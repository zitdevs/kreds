The KRED currency symbol — the $K. It sits next to balances, transactions,
rewards and prices the way `$` sits next to dollars, and is **never** used as a
logo, favicon or lockup. Size it to the cap height of the surrounding text
(13 for 13px mono, 18 for 20px, 24 for 26px); it aligns itself to the baseline.
